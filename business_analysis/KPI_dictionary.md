# ShopSphere Enterprise KPI Dictionary

## 1. Enterprise Metric Standards & Governance
This KPI Dictionary serves as the single authoritative governance standard across ShopSphere. Every metric is defined with its formal business purpose, mathematical formula, underlying data sources, reporting frequency, functional owner, and operational performance targets.

---

## 2. KPI Master Register

| KPI ID | KPI Name | Formal Definition | Mathematical Formula | Business Purpose | Data Source | Frequency | Functional Owner | Benchmark / Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KPI-001** | **Total Gross Revenue** | Pre-discount cumulative face value of all placed merchandise orders. | `SUM(quantity * unit_price)` | Evaluates raw commercial demand prior to promotional deductions. | `FactOrders` | Daily / Monthly | VP of Sales | $26.5M Baseline (Growth +15% YoY) |
| **KPI-002** | **Total Net Revenue** | Realized sales turnover after deducting promotional discounts. | `SUM(quantity * unit_price * (1 - discount))` | True financial top-line benchmark for executive planning and FP&A. | `FactOrders` | Daily / Monthly | CFO / Controller | $24.4M Baseline (Growth +12% YoY) |
| **KPI-003** | **Total Product Cost (COGS)** | Direct wholesale acquisition or manufacturing cost of units sold. | `SUM(quantity * unit_cost)` | Establishes cost floor required to fulfill physical merchandise volume. | `FactOrders`, `DimProduct` | Monthly | CFO / Head of Supply Chain | `< 68.0%` of Net Revenue |
| **KPI-004** | **Gross Profit** | Retained earnings generated from sales after deducting product COGS. | `Net Revenue - Total Product Cost` | Core profitability metric indicating commercial economic viability. | `FactOrders`, `DimProduct` | Daily / Monthly | CFO / Executive Team | $8.0M Baseline (Margin > 32%) |
| **KPI-005** | **Gross Profit Margin %** | Gross dollar profit expressed as a percentage of realized net revenue. | `(Gross Profit / Net Revenue) * 100` | Measures margin efficiency and highlights promotional margin erosion. | `FactOrders`, `DimProduct` | Daily / Monthly | VP of Merchandising | Target: `>= 33.0%` |
| **KPI-006** | **Total Completed Orders** | Aggregate count of unique transaction sales orders placed. | `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` | Primary indicator of operational throughput and platform transaction volume. | `FactOrders` | Daily / Monthly | VP of Operations | 100K+ Annual Orders |
| **KPI-007** | **Active Purchasing Customers** | Unique registered customer accounts with at least one finalized order. | `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)` | Evaluates active retail customer base and market penetration. | `FactOrders`, `DimCustomer` | Monthly | Head of Growth | Target: `> 25,000` Active Buyers |
| **KPI-008** | **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Average dollar expenditure realized per completed transaction. | `Net Revenue / Total Completed Orders` | Key revenue growth lever; targets basket expansion. | `FactOrders` | Daily / Monthly | VP of Merchandising | Target: `>= $240.00` ($238.58 Baseline) |
| **KPI-009** | **Product Return Rate %** | Percentage of placed orders resulting in an authorized customer return. | `(Returned Orders / Total Orders) * 100` | Quantifies reverse logistics friction and product/delivery dissatisfaction. | `FactOrders`, `FactReturns` | Weekly / Monthly | Director of Customer Experience | Target: `< 7.5%` (Current: 8.39%) |
| **KPI-010** | **Order Cancellation Rate %** | Percentage of customer orders aborted prior to delivery fulfillment. | `(Cancelled Orders / Total Orders) * 100` | Identifies checkout friction, payment failure, and customer remorse. | `FactOrders` | Daily / Weekly | E-commerce Operations Lead | Target: `< 4.0%` (Current: 4.77%) |
| **KPI-011** | **Repeat Customer Rate %** | Proportion of active customers who place 2 or more orders across lifetime. | `(Repeat Customers / Total Active Customers) * 100` | Primary gauge of brand stickiness, organic retention, and product fit. | `FactOrders`, `DimCustomer` | Monthly | Head of Retention Marketing | Target: `>= 55.0%` (Current: 55.60%) |
| **KPI-012** | **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | Cumulative net revenue generated per active purchasing customer account. | `Total Net Revenue / Active Customers` | Sets upper-bound economic threshold for Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). | `FactOrders`, `DimCustomer` | Quarterly | Growth Marketing Lead | Target: `>= $850.00` ($898.36 Baseline) |
| **KPI-013** | **Revenue Growth Rate % (MoM)** | Month-over-month percentage change in net sales turnover. | `((Rev_Current - Rev_Prior) / Rev_Prior) * 100` | Real-time commercial velocity and seasonal acceleration indicator. | `FactOrders`, `DimDate` | Monthly | CEO / Executive Team | Positive MoM Growth |
| **KPI-014** | **Profit Growth Rate % (YoY)** | Year-over-year annual percentage change in realized gross profit. | `((Profit_CY - Profit_PY) / Profit_PY) * 100` | Confirms whether bottom-line earnings are scaling alongside revenue. | `FactOrders`, `DimDate` | Annual / Quarterly | CFO / Board of Directors | Target: `>= +12.0%` YoY |
| **KPI-015** | **Late Delivery Rate %** | Percentage of shipments delivered past the promised checkout SLA date. | `(Late Delivered Shipments / Total Shipments) * 100` | Directly evaluates 3PL carrier SLA compliance and fulfillment efficiency. | `FactDelivery` | Daily / Weekly | VP of Supply Chain | Target: `< 5.0%` (Current: 11.38%) |
| **KPI-016** | **Average Delivery Delay** | Mean number of days delayed past promised SLA for tardy shipments. | `AVG(Actual Delivery Date - Promised Delivery Date)` | Measures the severity of delivery tardiness causing customer churn. | `FactDelivery` | Weekly / Monthly | 3PL Partner Manager | Target: `< 2.0 Days` (Current: 3.49 Days) |
