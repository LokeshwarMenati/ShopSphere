# ShopSphere Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)

## 1. Overview & Purpose
The Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) establishes bi-directional lineage across the entire project lifecycle, demonstrating rigorous Business Analyst methodology. It links high-level **Business Requirements (BR)** to **Functional Requirements (FR)**, **User Stories (US)**, **Acceptance Criteria (AC)**, **Power BI Dashboard Features**, **Key Performance Indicators (KPI)**, and **User Acceptance Test Cases (UAT)**.
